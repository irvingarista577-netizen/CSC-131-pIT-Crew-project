How to access the ACLS Skills Check Only?

You will soon be receiving an important email from RQI1stop.com. Please read and follow the directions to set up an account at https://cprlifeline.rqi1stop.com. You will use this account info to access the hands on skills check. With the Skills Check Only you must complete the AHA HeartCode Online course prior to attending the hands on skills check appointment .

***For any issues signing into RQI or have technical issues while taking the skills check, RQI's technical help line is: 1-800-594-9935.

RQI HeartCode Complete Program

You will perform the skills testing with a VAM (Voice Assisted Manikin). During the skills testing, an instructor will not be present since the manikin & computer will guide you through your skills. As a reminder, you must complete the HeartCode Online course before the hands on skills testing.

Again, an instructor will not be present, but you can call us during your skills testing if you have any questions or problems.

STEPS TO SUCCESSFULLY COMPLETE THIS PROGRAM:

HEARTCODE ONLINE COURSE:

*The HeartCode course must be completed prior to your skills appointment.

SKILLS TESTING:

*Arrive at the testing location, date, and time you signed up for. An instructor will not be present. The Voice Assisted Manikin (VAM) will guide you through the program.

*Log into your RQI account on computer attached to the manikin cart.

*Before starting each module, watch the recommended demonstration videos.

*After completing the skills session successfully, you will receive your AHA eCard.

*To access your eCard or CE credits anytime, you can log back into your RQI account.
